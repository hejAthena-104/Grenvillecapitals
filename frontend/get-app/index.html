Install on iPhone / iPad

1

Open Safari Browser

Navigate to our website using Safari. This feature only works in Safari, not Chrome or Firefox on iOS.

1

Open Chrome Browser

Navigate to our website using Google Chrome or any Chromium-based browser.

2

Look for the Install Banner

You may see an "Install App" banner at the bottom of the screen. Tap "Install" if you see it.

3

Or Use the Menu

If no banner appears, tap the three dots (⋮) in the top right corner, then select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

4

Confirm Installation

Tap "Install" in the popup dialog. The app will be added to your home screen and app drawer!

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our app and banking services

How do I enable push notifications?

To enable push notifications and stay updated on your account activity:

1. Install the app on your device following the instructions above
2. Log in to your account
3. After your second visit, you'll see a notification prompt asking if you'd like to enable notifications
4. Click "Allow" or "Enable Notifications" when prompted

If you missed the prompt, you can also enable notifications from your browser settings:

On Chrome: Click the lock icon in the address bar > Site settings > Notifications > Allow
On Safari (iOS): Go to Settings > Notifications > find our app > enable notifications
On Android: Go to Settings > Apps > find our app > Notifications > enable

Is the app safe to install?

Absolutely! Our app uses the same secure, encrypted connection as our website. All data is protected with bank-level SSL encryption, and we use multi-factor authentication to keep your account safe.

What notifications will I receive?

Once you enable notifications, you'll receive instant alerts for:

• Transfer approvals and rejections
• Support ticket updates and replies
• Loan application status changes
• Important account alerts and security notifications
• Low balance warnings

You can manage your notification preferences anytime in your Profile settings.

Can I use the app on multiple devices?

Yes! You can install and use the app on as many devices as you like - your phone, tablet, and computer. Your account syncs automatically across all devices, and you can enable notifications on each one to stay updated wherever you are.

Do I need to download from the App Store or Play Store?

No! Our app installs directly from your web browser - no app store required. This means:

• Faster installation - just a few taps
• No storage issues - uses minimal device storage
• Always up to date - you always get the latest version automatically
• No app store account needed - install without signing in anywhere

Simply follow the installation guide for your device above!

How do I uninstall the app?

On iPhone/iPad: Long-press the app icon, tap "Remove App", then "Delete App".

On Android: Long-press the app icon and drag to "Uninstall", or go to Settings > Apps.

On Desktop: Right-click the app in your taskbar or start menu and select "Uninstall".

How do I transfer money to another account?

To transfer money:

1. Log in to your account and go to "Transfer Funds"
2. Select the transfer type (domestic wire, international, or internal)
3. Enter the recipient's account details
4. Enter the amount and verify the details
5. Confirm with your transaction PIN

Transfers may require approval depending on the amount and your account settings.

How long do transfers take to process?

Transfer processing times vary by type:

• Internal transfers: Usually instant
• Domestic wire transfers: 1-2 business days
• International wire transfers: 3-5 business days
• ACH transfers: 2-3 business days

You'll receive a notification when your transfer is approved and when it's completed.

How do I apply for a loan?

Applying for a loan is easy:

1. Go to "Loans" from your dashboard
2. Click "Apply for a Loan"
3. Select your loan type (personal, auto, home, or business)
4. Enter the requested amount and loan term
5. Provide the purpose and any supporting documents
6. Submit your application

You'll receive notifications as your application progresses through review, approval, and disbursement.

What are the loan interest rates?

Our competitive interest rates vary by loan type and your credit profile. Current rates start from:

• Personal loans: From 6.99% APR
• Auto loans: From 4.99% APR
• Home loans: From 5.49% APR
• Business loans: From 7.49% APR

Visit our Rates page for the most current rates, or contact us for a personalized quote.

How do I change my password or PIN?

To change your password or transaction PIN:

1. Go to your Profile page
2. Click on the "Security" tab
3. Select "Change Password" or "Change PIN"
4. Enter your current credentials and new ones
5. Confirm the change

For security, you'll receive a notification whenever your password or PIN is changed.

What should I do if I notice unauthorized activity?

If you notice any suspicious or unauthorized activity on your account:

1. Change your password immediately from the Profile > Security section
2. Contact our support team through the Contact Us page or by phone
3. Review your recent transactions and note any you don't recognize
4. Open a support ticket to report the suspicious activity

Our security team will investigate and help protect your account. We're available 24/7 to assist you.

How do I contact customer support?

We offer multiple ways to get help:

• Support Tickets: Open a ticket from the "Contact Us" page for detailed inquiries
• Live Chat: Available during business hours
• Phone: Call our 24/7 customer service line
• Email: Send us an email and we'll respond within 24 hours

For fastest response, we recommend opening a support ticket through your account - you'll receive notifications when we reply.
