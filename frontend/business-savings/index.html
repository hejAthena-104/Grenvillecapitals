At StoneBridge Capitals, we understand the importance of building a strong personal relationship with our business customers. Our experienced staff will give you professional advice to assist you in making the right account choices to suit your financial needs.

Business Statement Savings

This is a great way to save your hard earned business dollars and increase your business potential. Earn interest on daily minimum ledger balances of $1,000 or more.

The minimum opening deposit for this account is $1,000.00.

Charges

A monthly maintenance fee of $10.00 will be assessed if a Calendar Month's Average Ledger balance is under $1,000.00.

You may make three (3) teller assisted withdrawals each calendar month; each subsequent withdrawal is $5.00.

See disclosure for details.

If you close your account within six (6) months from the opening date, an Early Closing fee of $10.00 will be assessed.
