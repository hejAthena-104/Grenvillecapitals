FAQs

If your question is not answered here, email support@grenvillecapitals.com and we will answer in writing.

Opening an account

An email address you can receive a code at, and a photo of a government ID. There is no minimum opening deposit.

We are required to verify who holds an account before money can be withdrawn. Your ID is used for that check and for nothing else. We do not sell customer information.

Moving money

From the deposits section of your dashboard. Choose the method, follow the instructions shown, and the deposit appears in your history as soon as it is confirmed.

Open transfers and choose whether the recipient is another Grenville Crest customer, an account at another bank, or international. Save the recipient and their details are filled in next time.

Money leaving Grenville Crest is held until it settles at the other end. Your available balance drops straight away so it always reflects what you can still spend.

Between our own accounts it is immediate. Out to another bank depends on that bank and the route. International takes longest. The status on the transaction is the accurate answer at any moment.

Security

With your email and password, plus a one-time code sent to your email. The code is needed every time.

Use the forgot-password link on the sign-in page. We email you a reset link. We cannot tell you your existing password because we do not store it in a readable form.

Never. Not by email, not by phone, not in any circumstance. Anyone who asks is not us. Our emails only ever come from an address ending @grenvillecapitals.com.
